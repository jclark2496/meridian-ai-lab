Company

Built in a garage.
Still repaired by hand.

Meridian Outfitters has been making and mending outdoor goods since 1998 — first for friends, then for anyone who measures a year in miles.

We sell fewer things, made better, meant to go farther.

The first Meridian pieces were cut on a folding table behind a Fort Worth bait shop. Canvas that could take a Texas hailstorm. Leather that softened instead of cracking. Hardware we could replace in the field.

That standard never changed. Every product still passes three seasons of staff use before it ships. If we wouldn’t take it ourselves, it doesn’t make the line.

Today we run twelve flagship stores and a repair bench that has saved more jackets than we can count. The garage is gone. The promise is not.
